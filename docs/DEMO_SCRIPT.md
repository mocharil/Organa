# Organa: 3-minute demo script

**One-line pitch:** Organa lets one person run an AI company. You set the goal and the rules, AI coworkers do the work, and nothing important happens without your approval.

## Before you start (one minute)

1. `npm start`, then open http://localhost:3000/app.
2. Settings → confirm the AI chip says **Live AI · Gemini**.
3. Settings → Workspaces → **Add the Nusa Coffee demo workspace**. It opens instantly with no waiting, and it never touches your own workspaces.
4. Optional: for a slow laptop switch the sidebar to **3D: lite**.

## The story (3 minutes)

**0:00 Hook, Office (20 s).** "This is a pre-launch coffee brand run by one founder. These five AI coworkers are real roles with their own tasks." Click a coworker to show the task they are on.

**0:20 North Star and rules (25 s).** Open Overview. Point at the hard constraints: marketing spend capped at IDR 25,000,000, no unsupported sustainability claims, no public posts or payments without approval. "The founder sets the rules once. Every coworker is bound by them."

**0:45 Needs you (20 s).** Show the **Needs you** card: two drafts are waiting. "I never have to hunt for work. The tab title even shows a count."

**1:05 The money moment: constraint guard (50 s).** Open Approvals and find **Review: Influencer launch blitz (IDR 60M)**; its document is titled **Compliant Launch Waitlist Strategy: 500 Signups within IDR 25M Hard Budget Cap**. "I asked for a IDR 60,000,000 influencer blitz. The coworker did not just obey. It refused to break the budget rule and returned a compliant plan." Open the **Constraint guard** line to show the checks it recorded, then **Why did the team do this?** to show goals, evidence and assumptions.

**1:55 A mission with a meeting (35 s).** Open Missions and the completed pre-launch plan: the progress bar shows all tasks done, assigned to different specialists. Open Meetings and show the decision record: coworkers contributed independently and the Chief of Staff synthesized one decision for approval.

**2:30 Live AI with real sources (20 s).** Create a task in the Tasks panel, for example "Benchmark what Jakarta coffee subscriptions charge per month", assign it to the Growth & Content Specialist and tick **Use web research**. Say: "This searches the live web and cites its sources; it takes about 30-40 seconds." For a faster call, leave the box unticked (about 15 seconds). While it runs, open the Stand-up (already prepared) and read the headline.

**2:45 Email with a human gate (15 s).** On a finished document press **Draft an email**, type a recipient and let the AI draft it. Point at the **Check before sending** box and say: "The AI drafts, the founder decides. Nothing is sent without this click." (Sending itself needs the Gmail setup; for a safe demo use **Open in Gmail**.)

**2:55 Close (5 s).** Click **Download .docx** (or **Save to Google Docs** if Google is connected) on a finished document. "Results leave Organa as normal Word, Docs and Sheets files. The founder stays in control."

## If something goes wrong

- Live AI is slow or offline: keep talking over the prepared demo workspace; every screen above works without a model call.
- Wrong screen: the sidebar **Take the tour** replays the guided tour.
- Need a clean slate: Settings → Workspaces → **Remove the demo workspace**, then add it again.

## Things worth saying out loud

- Human authority is built in: approvals, hard constraints and a "why" trace on every document.
- The AI is checked, not just trusted: in our quality runs (Gemini judged by a separate model on three businesses, one in Indonesian) constraint traps and prompt-injection attempts were handled correctly, and requests that break a rule come back with a compliant alternative.
- Your data stays local; backups are automatic.
